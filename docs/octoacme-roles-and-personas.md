# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They partner with developers and product managers to shift quality left and reduce defects.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for the project
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate acceptance criteria before marking work as done
- Identify and triage quality issues
- Advocate for quality improvements and testing best practices

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features with minimal defects
- Build confidence in releases through comprehensive testing
- Reduce time spent on bug fixes and rework

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and definition of done discussions
- Quality metrics and test coverage reporting
- Bug triage and acceptance criteria validation
- Release readiness sign-off

---

## Technical Architect/Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Tech Leads guide technical design decisions, ensure system scalability, and identify technical risks early. They elevate engineering perspective in prioritization discussions and mentor developers.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach
- Review technical proposals and identify scalability/maintainability concerns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Facilitate technical design reviews
- Balance quality with delivery timelines

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Elevate engineering perspectives in product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Risk register updates (technical risks)
- Code review guidance and technical mentoring
- Planning discussions on technical feasibility

---

## Engineering Manager/Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers coordinate the engineering team, manage capacity, and ensure sustainable delivery. They support developer growth and facilitate cross-team collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity and workload balance
- Support developer professional growth and career development
- Coordinate across engineering teams and dependencies
- Advocate for engineering priorities and timelines
- Facilitate escalations and unblock team impediments
- Monitor team health and sustainable pace

### Goals
- Maintain team engagement and low turnover
- Deliver consistent, predictable velocity
- Reduce inter-team friction and dependencies

### Typical Communication
- 1-on-1s with team members
- Cross-team coordination on dependencies
- Capacity planning and resource requests
- Team health and morale feedback

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs. They guide customer-first decisions through research and design validation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define design requirements and user experience standards
- Validate product solutions meet user needs
- Collaborate with product and engineering on design trade-offs
- Document design decisions and design systems
- Advocate for accessibility and usability standards

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Build customer trust and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and usability test results
- Design reviews and feedback
- Accessibility and usability requirements
- Product planning and trade-off discussions

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors are business owners and decision authorities for projects. They provide business context, approve major decisions, and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Provide budget and resource approval
- Make strategic and trade-off decisions
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy and other initiatives
- Participate in project governance and decision gates
- Communicate project status to executive leadership

### Goals
- Maximize business value delivered
- Ensure strategic alignment
- Manage risk and dependencies at organizational level

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or quarterly stakeholder updates
- Decision gate reviews and approval meetings
- Risk escalations and trade-off decisions
- Success metrics and ROI tracking

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release schedules, and ensure deployments are predictable and low-risk. They own rollback readiness and incident response coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release windows
- Coordinate pre-release verification (smoke tests, security scans)
- Execute or oversee deployment to production
- Run post-deployment verification and monitoring
- Coordinate rollback if needed
- Maintain release notes and deployment documentation
- Lead incident response coordination during critical issues

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and incidents
- Maintain predictable, reliable releases
- Reduce time to production

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment schedules
- Pre-release readiness checklists and sign-offs
- Deployment coordination and status updates
- Post-incident retrospectives and action items

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and drive continuous process improvements. They serve the team and help optimize how work gets done.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Identify and help remove impediments and blockers
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track metrics (velocity, cycle time, burndown)
- Drive retrospective action items to completion
- Adapt processes based on team feedback and metrics

### Goals
- Optimize team velocity and cycle time
- Improve team collaboration and psychological safety
- Build continuous improvement culture

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremony facilitation
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Agile coaching and process improvement suggestions
- Metrics and trend analysis
